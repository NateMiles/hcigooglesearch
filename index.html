Vibe Search: Final Prototype

A medium-fidelity redesign of searching Google Maps for a place by its qualities. It combines plain-language search with review evidence (design B) and one-tap vibe and feature chips (design A). The log on the right counts taps and time until the participant starts directions. Places, reviews and photos are made up.

Your task

Find a cozy coffee shop with good food and free books

Use the phone to find a nearby coffee shop that is cozy, has good food and has free books. Check that it really has free books, then start directions to it. Type, tap the microphone or tap the chips: whatever feels natural.

Task for the participant

Find a cozy coffee shop with good food and free books

Ask the participant to find a nearby coffee shop that is cozy, has good food and has free books to read. They should check that it really has free books, then start directions. They can type a request, tap the microphone, or tap the chips.
